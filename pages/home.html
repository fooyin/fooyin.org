About

fooyin is a music player built around customisation. Arrange its widgets into an interface that suits you and manage large local collections. Plugins add new functionality, while FooScript provides advanced formatting, queries and autoplaylists.

Developed and supported on Linux and Windows, with build support for macOS.

Made for your music

  • Customisable interface Start with a preset or build your own layout from a blank canvas.
  • Library and playlists Organise, search and filter large collections with flexible views.
  • Broad format support Play major audio formats, archives and embedded or external CUE sheets.
  • Plugins and FooScript Extend fooyin and create advanced display logic, queries and autoplaylists.
  • Rich music tools Work with lyrics, artwork, tags, ReplayGain and detailed metadata.
  • Desktop integrations Use media controls, scrobbling services and Discord Rich Presence.
